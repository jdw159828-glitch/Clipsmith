Common questions

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything — trimming, compression, audio extraction, and subtitle recognition — runs on your iPhone. There is no account and no server.
Where are my exported files?
In My Files inside the app. Videos can also be saved to Photos; audio and subtitle files can be saved to the Files app or shared. Deleting the app deletes the files kept inside it, so save anything you want to keep.
What does the free version include?
Trim, compress, extract audio, and extract subtitles as TXT, for clips up to 60 seconds. Trim a longer video to 60 seconds and it can be exported for free. Pro removes the length limit and adds SRT/VTT subtitles, canvas ratios, and batch processing.
Subtitles say my language isn't available.
Subtitles use your iPhone's on-device dictation, so only languages your device can recognise offline are offered. Turn on Dictation in Settings → General → Keyboard, and make sure the language is added under Keyboards. Clipsmith never sends audio to a server to work around this.
The subtitles have mistakes.
Speech recognition is never perfect, especially with music, background noise, or several people talking at once. Review the text before exporting; TXT, SRT, and VTT files can all be edited afterwards.
I paid but the app still shows the free limits.
Open Go Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase.
How do I cancel a subscription or free trial?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by this app: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge.
Can Clipsmith download videos from TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram?
No, and it won't. Clipsmith only works with videos already on your device.
